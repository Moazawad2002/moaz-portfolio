Professional Background

Where Accounting Rigor Meets Software Engineering

Building business software requires more than knowing how to code; it requires understanding chart of accounts, credit exposure, gross margins, and inventory lifecycles.

I am a Business Systems Developer graduating from Egypt-Japan University For Science and Technology (E-JUST, Class of 2025) with a degree in Accounting and Information Systems. This dual foundation enables me to design software solutions that directly reflect commercial reality and eliminate operational vulnerabilities.

Most operational errors in growing businesses occur because off-the-shelf software doesn't fit custom commercial workflows, while pure software developers often misunderstand inventory valuation (WAC/FIFO), credit risk limits, and double-entry transaction ledgers.

My Core Philosophy: Business systems must guarantee data integrity at the database layer. By enforcing business rules—such as deterministic stock availability, discount authorization gates, and multi-tenant row security—I eliminate revenue leaks and deliver frictionless tools for field teams and executives alike.
